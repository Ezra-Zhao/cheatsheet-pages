How it works

No app for your customers to install. They just message your WhatsApp Business number, the way they already do.

Step 1

The customer messages you

They type their car model and year — for example, "2019 Camry" — in English, Chinese, or Spanish.

Step 2

WhatsServe answers automatically

It looks up the correct wiper size for that car and replies with the size and the price from your price list.

Step 3

The customer comes to you

The reply points them to your shop to buy or get the wipers fitted. Anything the bot can't answer is handed to you, with the conversation attached.

Hi, do you have wipers for a 2019 Camry?
Yes. Your 2019 Camry takes a 28" driver side and 18" passenger side. The pair is $XX at our shop — you can pick them up today, or we can fit them for you here.
Great, what time do you close?
That question is answered from your shop's own FAQ — hours, address, payment, fitting — set up with you during setup.

Example only. Prices and answers come from your own price list and shop details.

Questions shops ask us

What do I need to start?

A WhatsApp Business number and your wiper price list. If you don't have a WhatsApp Business number yet, getting one connected is part of the setup. Your price list can be a spreadsheet, a document, or clear photos of your price sheets.

How long until it's live?

Setup is done remotely and asynchronously — you send us your details, we configure everything, then you test the bot as if you were a customer. It only goes live on your number after you've tried it and approved the answers.

I'm not technical. Is this going to be a headache?

No. There is nothing for you to install or maintain. You answer a few questions about your shop, send your price list, and test the result by messaging it. If a reply sounds wrong, tell us in plain words and we adjust it.

Can I try it before it goes live?

Yes — that's built into every tier. Before launch you message the bot yourself, ask it the things your customers ask, and check the sizes, prices, and wording. We fix what needs fixing, and only then does it start answering your customers.

What if a reply needs fixing after launch?

Every package includes one free revision round after launch. We go through the bot's real conversations with your customers together, and fix the replies that missed — the questions it got wrong, the wording that doesn't sound like your shop. That round is on us.
