Replace Google One 2TB ($100/yr) with Immich — save $100 a year

Photo and video backup with automatic phone uploads, AI search and face recognition — on your own hard drives.

Prices are the official prices the cheatsheet verified in October 2026, on annual billing; SaaS prices change often, so check the official site before you act. Self-hosting needs one small server: an old PC, a Raspberry Pi, a NAS, or a cloud VPS.

mkdir immich && cd immich && wget -O docker-compose.yml https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/latest/download/docker-compose.yml && wget -O .env https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/latest/download/example.env && docker compose up -d

Good to know

After it starts, open http://localhost:2283 and connect the mobile app for automatic backup.
